## Context
The layer needs teeth in CI/hooks without blocking exploration.

## Decision
Run deterministic checks whose severity is driven by config rules; exit non-zero on errors.

## Why
Configurable rules let teams choose facilitate-and-verify vs strict, matching the chosen compliance model.

## Consequences
Full 'every function tagged' enforcement needs per-language lint; out of scope for v1.
