## Context
The system should improve from past decisions and code.

## Decision
Let agents record explicit learnings, and provide a deterministic distill that summarizes recurring tags and reversals.

## Why
A hybrid keeps high-quality human/agent insight while guaranteeing a useful baseline with no LLM dependency.

## Consequences
Mechanical distillation is shallow; richer insight requires an agent.
