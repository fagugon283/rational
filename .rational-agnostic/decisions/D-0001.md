## Context
Users work in many languages, but artifacts must be portable across agents of different vendors and over time.

## Decision
Store every decision, task, memory and learning in English; keep the user's working locale only in config for UI.

## Why
A single canonical language keeps the knowledge base searchable, diffable and readable by any agent, and avoids mixed-language drift.

## Consequences
Agents must translate their reasoning into English before persisting.
