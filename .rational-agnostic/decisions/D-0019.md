## Context
Users must 'hash a function and find why it was written'.

## Decision
tag_code takes the symbol body so the SHA is exact; why resolves forward and reverse, including by hash.

## Why
Caller-supplied bodies avoid fragile cross-language parsing while enabling exact hash lookup.

## Consequences
Anchoring is explicit; untagged symbols are invisible to reverse lookup.
