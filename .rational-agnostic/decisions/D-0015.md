## Context
Vectors are large, binary-ish and provider-specific.

## Decision
Treat the vector store as derived data; markdown files remain the source of truth; rebuild on demand or on embedder mismatch.

## Why
Keeps git clean and portable, and lets users switch embedding providers by reindexing.

## Consequences
Search may trigger a rebuild on first use or after provider changes.
