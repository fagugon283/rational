## Context
Records are human-editable markdown with structured metadata.

## Decision
Parse/serialize with gray-matter behind a tiny typed wrapper.

## Why
Frontmatter keeps records readable and diffable while carrying typed metadata; a wrapper isolates the dependency.

## Consequences
Couples the format to YAML frontmatter conventions.
