## Context
Decisions are the heart of the system and must be readable and diffable.

## Decision
Store each decision as its own markdown file with a fixed section template.

## Why
One-file-per-decision follows the established ADR pattern, keeps git history granular and is easy for agents to create.

## Consequences
Many small files in large projects; fine for git.
