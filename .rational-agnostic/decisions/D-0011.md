## Context
Agents must cheaply know each task's state.

## Decision
Persist full task detail in per-task files and mirror status into board.json.

## Why
The board gives a cheap, token-light status overview without reading every task file.

## Consequences
Board can drift if edited by hand; it is rewritten on every status change.
