## Context
Parallel agents and crashes could corrupt JSON indexes or markdown records.

## Decision
Write to a temp sibling then rename over the target.

## Why
Rename is atomic on a filesystem, so a reader never sees a partial file.

## Consequences
Leaves rare temp files on hard crash; negligible.
