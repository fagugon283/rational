## Context
We need to detect when a tagged function meaningfully changed from its decision.

## Decision
Normalize whitespace/blank lines, then SHA-256 the result.

## Why
Reformatting should not register as drift, but semantic edits should; SHA-256 is standard and collision-resistant.

## Consequences
Comment-only or rename changes inside the body still change the hash.
