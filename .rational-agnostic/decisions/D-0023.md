## Context
Humans and non-MCP agents need access, and CI needs verify.

## Decision
Build a commander CLI that only wires flags to core functions, with a --json mode.

## Why
Keeping the CLI thin ensures CLI and MCP never diverge and simplifies testing.

## Consequences
Interactive-free design means complex inputs use many flags.
