## Context
Tags must be found across languages without a parser per language.

## Decision
Regex-scan all source files for @why and guess the nearby symbol; skip markdown/doc and binary files.

## Why
A single regex works everywhere; skipping markdown avoids flagging documentation examples as orphan tags.

## Consequences
Symbol guessing is heuristic; good enough for coverage and orphan checks.
