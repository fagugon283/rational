## Context
Multiple agents from different vendors create records concurrently.

## Decision
Decisions get collision-checked random hex ids; tasks and learnings get sequential ids allocated under a lock.

## Why
Random ids let parallel agents mint decision ids with no central counter contention; sequential ids suit human-facing task boards where ordering matters.

## Consequences
Decision ids are not chronologically ordered.
